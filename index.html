The hard day. Three drivers, rotate every two hours, eat in the truck where you can. You will hate Friday. This is normal.

Sleep on Calgary's west side, pointed at Hwy 1. Cochrane is 30 miles further and saves you half an hour tomorrow, but it's a small town on a long weekend — book early or don't bother.

Detour Dossier · Hour Two, Thursday

Rockford

Total exposure: 11 minutes

Settlers arrived in 1834 at a rocky spot where the Rock River could be forded and named it Rockford. That was the high-water mark of local creativity and everything since has been downhill.

It became the Screw Capital of the World — bolts and fasteners. A city whose entire identity was hardware, which it then lost.

On January 2, 1920, federal agents, army intelligence and local police swept the factories, union halls, taverns and pool halls in a Palmer Raid — the largest mass arrest in the city's history, 180 people, nearly all foreign-born, hauled in as suspected "alien radicals." Rockford's contribution to American civil liberties is a mass roundup of immigrants.

  • 1981Amtrak's Black Hawk runs for the last time on September 30. Rockford loses passenger rail to Chicago and hasn't had it back in 45 years.
  • 1982Unemployment hits 26%.
  • 1983Per-capita income rank among 305 largest US metros falls from 44th to 131st in four years.
  • 1993Money magazine ranks it the single worst place to live among America's 300 largest cities.
  • 2010Unemployment peaks again at 16%.
  • 2013Forbes names it #3 Most Miserable City in America — behind only Detroit and Flint, and ahead of Chicago at #4.
  • NowUS News ranks it the most dangerous city in Illinois and top-25 nationally. Winnebago County carries some of the highest effective property taxes in the country.

It out-miserabled the city you are driving out of your way to avoid. It beat Chicago at being bad. That is the whole story in one data point.

The most famous thing bearing the name is The Rockford Files, a detective show set entirely in Malibu, California. Even the fictional Rockford wanted out.

Do not stop for gas. Belvidere or Beloit.

Tue · Sept 8

Talbot Lake Fishing Day

Full day rowboat rental · ~45 min from cabin

Self-guided rowboat rental, Curries Guiding. Crystal-clear water at 4.5 ft deep — see the bottom, weeds, fish. Stable boat, easy to handle. Bring your cooler, booze, snacks, fishing gear.

  • Boat rental: 12 hours ~$163 (pickup at Astoria Hotel in Jasper)
  • Optional electric motor: $50 (saves your arms)
  • What's included: Oars, PFDs, rope, safety whistle, equipment orientation
  • Bring: Parks Canada fishing licence (buy at Astoria, ~$20), your own fishing gear
  • Max 4 people per boat — you could rent two boats for the whole group

Quiet lake, no tour boats, no crowds. Spectacular views and solid fishing. This is your day off from hiking.

Wed · Sept 9

Mount Robson, BC · Big Hike Day

~155 miles round trip

West into BC. Provincial park, free entry. BC is Pacific time — gain an hour going, lose it back.

  • HIKE: Kinney Lake — 5.4 miles round trip, ~1,410 ft gain. Wide, near-flat old road to a turquoise glacial lake. The gentlest five miles in the Rockies — no steep pitches anywhere. Right in your range.
  • Overlander Falls — 1 mile round trip add-on

Robson is 12,972 ft and hides in cloud two days out of three. When it's out, most imposing mountain view on the continent.

Thu · Sept 10

~90 miles round trip
  • Pocahontas ghost town — flat, easy interpretive loop through early-1900s coal settlement

  • Optional Sulphur Skyline — 8 km / 5 mi, 700m / 2,300 ft, relentlessly steep. Only if anyone wants to punish themselves.

Lake Louise & Moraine

~120 miles round trip
No shuttle, no Moraine Lake. Road closed to private vehicles since 2023. Lake Louise parking gone by 7am. This is the reservation you set the Sept 10 alarm for.

Lake Louise Park & Ride at the ski resort — 1 hour from Canmore. Arrive 20 min before your window. Day pass at gate.

Moraine Lake first:

  • The Rockpile — 2,000 ft, some stairs. Old $20-bill view. Non-negotiable.
  • Consolation Lakes — 3.7 miles round trip, 213 ft gain. Quiet, near-flat, boulder field and lake. Best real hike K-G and a baby carrier can both do.

Free Lake Connector to Lake Louise:

  • Lakeshore — 2.5 miles round trip, flat, everyone
  • Lake Agnes Tea House — 4.4 miles round trip, 1,300 ft. Real 1901 tea house. Cash only.
  • Larch Valley for the strong — 5.4 miles, 1,755 ft. Larches peak Sept 18–25. Check trailhead sign — sometimes minimum group of 4 for bear safety.
K-G will announce he could do Larch Valley. He will then see 1,750 feet of switchbacks and his foot will develop opinions.

Mon · Sept 14

Yoho National Park

~110 miles round trip

Last full day with everyone. 1h20 from Canmore. Day pass covers it. Perfect mixed-ability day — enormous payoff, almost no elevation.

  • Takakkaw Falls — 1,260 ft. Tight switchbacks on access road, fine if you go slow. Flat 0.8 mile walk to falls. Road closes early October — you're just inside.
  • Emerald Lake loop — 3.2 miles, flat, most absurdly coloured lake in the Rockies. Mostly stroller-passable.
  • Natural Bridge — five minutes
  • Spiral Tunnels — trains loop through the mountain and cross over themselves at two elevations

The K-G foot protocol

Miette once, Banff Upper Hot Springs, Moose Jaw mineral soak, a hot tub at the condo most nights, and one more soak — six total. Friday the 4th is the one day he'll actually need them the most.

Two real hiking days (Maligne Bald Hills + Mount Robson Kinney Lake) with a fishing break in between. Gentle options on every other day. Nobody sits out and nobody waits around.

Bring trekking poles. They take real load off one foot on descents, which is where the damage happens. He'll refuse them for two days and use them for the rest of the trip.
