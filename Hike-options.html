Out the door

5–15 min

Hinton's own trail network. For the afternoon nobody feels like driving anywhere.

Switzer Provincial Park

20–22 min

North on Highway 40. A provincial park sitting right next door to a national park is the best crowd-avoidance trick in this corner of Alberta.

Jasper east gate

30 min

Inside the park, at the end almost nobody drives to. Park pass required here — day passes only, no need for the annual.

Miette Road & the Brûlé backroad

40–50 min

The dead-end road up to the hot springs, plus one gravel detour past Brûlé. Soaking pools at the end of the Miette road make this the best-value drive on the list.

Jasper town side

55–60 min

The full hour, and worth it at least twice. Note for 2026: Maligne Canyon and the Cavell Road are closed for the whole season, so ignore any Top 10 list that leads with them — the lakes benchland behind town is where the locals go anyway.

One we're skipping

Old Man Trail via Roche Bonhomme: 5.6 miles with 4,363 feet of gain. That is not a hike, that is a dare, and we have a man with a foot situation and a documented tendency to narrate his own knees.
